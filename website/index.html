How it plays

Start with a rusty Pigeon. Work your way up.

Orders ring on a phone in the corner of your screen. Drive to the pickup, stop for a few seconds, then take it to the customer. Everything happens on the real map BeamNG.Earth builds around you.

01 · COURIER

Food and groceries

Meals, family orders and grocery runs from real restaurants and shops. Carry up to three orders at once. Drive gently: a hard knock spoils the food.

02 · COMPANY WORK

Borrow the company van

Two deliveries from a depot, then back to the yard for your wages. Good work builds reputation and unlocks contracts in your own van.

03 · FREIGHT

Vans, box trucks, semis

Real cargo containers and loads. Finish 5 van jobs to unlock the box truck, then 10 more for the articulated truck.

Your career

One phone runs it all.

Your money, car and progress are saved locally, with backups. Prices follow what deliveries pay, so a new part or a better car always means a few more shifts.

  • GarageRepairs, used cars with real mileage and wear, and real BeamNG parts bought through the game's own Vehicle Config.
  • HomePick an address, drive there and claim the parking spot. It holds up to three cars.
  • DebtRepairing for free through the game's menu adds debt. Half of each delivery pays it off.
  • DriversAfter 30 deliveries, hire drivers for your parked cars. They earn while you work.
  • AnywhereCzechia is built in. Elsewhere, a small helper program finds nearby restaurants, shops and addresses from Overture Maps.

Install

Ready in a few minutes.

You need BeamNG.drive and BeamNG.Earth installed and working first.

Latest release Full guide

  1. Quick install on Windows

    Open PowerShell and paste this. It installs the mod and the helper from the latest release. You can also do it by hand with the next steps.

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/themazzy/cargo-earth/main/install.ps1 | iex

  2. Or add the mod by hand

    Close the game. Put the downloaded cargo_earth_career_*.zip into the mods folder of your BeamNG user folder. Leave it zipped and keep only one version.

  3. Run the helper (outside Czechia, and for grocery runs)

    Install Python 3, then the two libraries the helper needs. Run the helper from the release and keep it open while you play.

    python -m pip install duckdb fsspec

  4. Add the phone

    Load a BeamNG.Earth world, open the UI apps editor and add Cargo Earth: Phone. Start your career from the phone.

Questions

Before you head out

Is it finished?

No. It is in early development and tested mostly outside the game. Expect rough edges, and please report what you find.

Does it replace BeamNG's own career mode?

No. Cargo Earth is its own career on top of BeamNG.Earth. It is not made by or affiliated with BeamNG or BeamNG.Earth.

Where do the places come from?

From Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap. Restaurants and depots are real places, but the jobs and companies in the game are made up. A map point is not always a perfect stopping spot, so you can cancel a company job at a bad location without a fee.

What does the helper send over the internet?

It only downloads public map data from Overture Maps. It runs on your own computer and does not upload your save or position anywhere.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub with the mod version, what you did, and the lines tagged cargoEarth from beamng.log. Remove your home address before sharing a save.
